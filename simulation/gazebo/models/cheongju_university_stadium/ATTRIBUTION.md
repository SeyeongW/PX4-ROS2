# Reference and asset notice

This model is original procedural SDF geometry generated by
`../../gen_cju_stadium_model.py`. It contains no downloaded photograph,
texture, logo, STL, DAE, OBJ, or other third-party mesh.

The visual arrangement was informed by:

- Cheongju University, [Main Complex Stadium facility page](https://www.cju.ac.kr/www/contents.do?key=6858)
- Cheongju University, [campus map / building 40](https://www.cju.ac.kr/site/campusmap/sub.jsp)
- Cheongju University, [70-year history](https://www.cju.ac.kr/DATA/download/history/cts5453_file7.pdf)
- Cheongju University, [official ground-level VR](https://www.cju.ac.kr/common/vr/ground/sports_complex/index.html)
- [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), for
  approximate location, orientation, and footprint context (ODbL)

University photographs, VR imagery, and campus-map artwork are references only
and are not redistributed. Dimensions and visual details not explicitly
published by the sources are documented as approximations in
`../../CJU_STADIUM_WORLD.md`.
